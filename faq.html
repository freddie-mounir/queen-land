Store Queen Land fertilizer products in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and moisture. Keep bags sealed tightly and off the ground on pallets or platforms. Protect from rain and humidity to prevent caking or degradation. Store away from incompatible materials, especially fuels, oxidizers, and pesticides. Follow the specific storage instructions on each product label, and keep products out of reach of children and unauthorized persons.
